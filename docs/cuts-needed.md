# Connectors still to cut

> **Scope (Josh, Nov 15 2026; it has been lost before, keep it):** the base body is mostly Mar Fil, with weapons and customisation added on top. **Terminators and Gravis are not part of this system**: there is no expectation that they work in it, as they would need their own base fits (their own torsos, sockets and joints). They, and Marta Phobos, are left out here. **Plain arms: Mar Fil only.** From the other makers only their two-handed weapons and weapons with a hand attached (a specific posed arm may be added by name later); Marta's legs stay in for their unique poses.

Generated by `kits/connectors/cuts_needed.py` from the page index and the written `Seat connectors` runs; run it again after a cut. One cut a maker folder and joint first: the search then shows which kits share it (Mar Fil's Veteran / Bladeguard neck turned out to be its own), and only those that don't need a cut of their own. A pad or shin pad needs no cut: place one on the arm's pad spot / the legs' shin spot and its inside is taken from it. 

**Weapons with a hand: one cut per wrist style, 3 in all, for every maker** (Josh, Nov 15: there are 3 wrist styles in the files; the fit is the mating surface and the roll, the rest of the mesh doesn't matter). On one weapon of each style, sat on a Mar Fil arm with that wrist as it should be held, cut **only the wrist end** (what meets the arm), not the fingers or the hand around it. The search then finds that wrist end on every weapon of every maker. Two-handed pieces that carry their arms go by the shoulder disc instead.
