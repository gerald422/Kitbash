> **Scope (Josh, Nov 15 2026; it has been lost before, keep it):** the base body is mostly Mar Fil, with weapons and customisation added on top. **Terminators and Gravis are not part of this system**: there is no expectation that they work in it, as they would need their own base fits (their own torsos, sockets and joints). They, and Marta Phobos, are left out here. **Plain arms: Mar Fil only.** From the other makers only their two-handed weapons and weapons with a hand attached (a specific posed arm may be added by name later); Marta's legs stay in for their unique poses.

Generated by `kits/connectors/cuts_needed.py` from the page index and the written `Seat connectors` runs; run it again after a cut. One cut a maker folder and joint first: the search then shows which kits share it (Mar Fil's Veteran / Bladeguard neck turned out to be its own), and only those that don't need a cut of their own. A pad or shin pad needs no cut: place one on the arm's pad spot / the legs' shin spot and its inside is taken from it. **Weapons with a hand and backpacks: one cut per design** (Josh, Nov 15: everything fitted against the matching part, no old fits kept). Sit one weapon of the design on a Mar Fil arm's wrist as it should be held (one backpack on a Mar Fil torso's back) and cut the part of it that meets the arm (the torso): the search then finds every part with that hand (mount). The designs below come from a dry run that sorted the parts that had a fit by their hand sculpt; parts with no fit could not be sorted, so cut the biggest designs first and the search shows what is left.
